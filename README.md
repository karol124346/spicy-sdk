## 🧭 Your First Project

Now that the software is open, here is the simplest possible way to create something.

1. **Choose what you want to make.** Decide between an image, a video or a piece of text. Each one behaves a little differently — images appear almost instantly, videos take longer because more frames need to be drawn.
2. **Write a short description.** Be plain and specific. "A red bicycle leaning against a brick wall in the rain" works far better than "bike". The more detail you give, the closer the result matches the picture in your head.
3. **Send the request.** Press the button or key that starts the job. The software sends your description to SpicyAPI through the single endpoint and waits for a reply.
4. **Collect your result.** When it is ready, the finished image, video or text is handed back to you. Save it wherever you keep your files.
5. **Try again with a tweak.** Change one word and send it once more. Small adjustments are the fastest way to learn what the system responds to.

There is no penalty for experimenting. Run the same description twice and you will get slight variations, which is often exactly what you want when hunting for the perfect result.

## 🎨 What You Can Create

The four abilities built into spicy-sdk cover most creative needs:

**Image generation.** Turn a written description into a finished picture. Useful for concept art, thumbnails, illustrations, mock-ups and mood boards.

**Text generation.** Ask for a paragraph, a product description, a caption or a short story. The system writes it out in clear language you can copy straight into your own work.

**Image-to-video.** Give the software a still picture and it produces a moving clip from it. This is perfect for bringing a poster or a photo to life.

**Text-to-video.** Describe a scene in words and receive a short video of it. This is the most demanding feature, so give it a little more time and a clearer description than you would for a still image.

Because all four sit behind the same connection, switching between them is just a matter of asking for something different. No reinstalling, no new accounts, no second download.

## ⚙️ How It Works

Think of spicy-sdk as a messenger. You hand it a note describing what you want. It walks to SpicyAPI, hands over the note, waits, and brings back the finished piece. You never have to know the route the messenger takes.

Under the surface, your description is packaged into a request and sent securely. The service does the heavy processing on its own machines — your computer is not doing the rendering, so a modest laptop is perfectly adequate. When the work is finished, the result travels back to you and is presented in the folder or window you are already looking at.

This design is why the same tool can handle such different jobs. The messenger does not care whether the note asks for a picture or a paragraph; it simply delivers and returns.

## 🛠️ Common Questions

**Do I need to be a programmer?**
No. The download and setup steps on this page are all you need. The code inside is written for developers, but using the finished application does not require reading it.

**Will it slow down my computer?**
No. The demanding work happens on the SpicyAPI servers, not on your machine. Your computer mainly passes messages back and forth.

**Why did my video take longer than my image?**
Video contains many frames, each of which must be generated and stitched together. A short clip is normal to wait for; a long one naturally takes more time.
